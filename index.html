01 / CAPABILITIES

Small machine.
Big possibilities.

A compact motion platform where mechanical structure and electronic control work together to reproduce lines, letters and illustrations on a surface.

FEATURE / 01
⌖

Dual-axis movement

Coordinated X and Y movement positions the pen across the drawing area using stepper-driven motion.

FEATURE / 02
✳

Pen lift control

A servo-based mechanism can raise or lower the pen to move between drawing and non-drawing paths.

FEATURE / 03
⌁

Digital path execution

Designed to interpret plotted paths and convert instructions into controlled machine movement.

FEATURE / 04
▦

Guided linear motion

Guide rods and a moving carriage help keep the pen assembly aligned during operation.

FEATURE / 05
⌘

Microcontroller based

An Arduino-class controller coordinates motor drivers, end-stop inputs and the pen-lift servo.

FEATURE / 06
✧

Creative applications

Explore lettering, geometric patterns, diagrams, signatures and line-art experiments.

03 / WORKFLOW

From file
to finished line.

A simple view of the intended workflow. The exact software pipeline can be updated to match the firmware and plotting software used in your final build.

STEP 01 / INPUT

Create a design

Prepare lettering, a drawing or a vector path on a digital device.

STEP 02 / CONVERT

Prepare toolpath

Convert the design into movement instructions supported by the controller.

STEP 03 / EXECUTE

Move with control

The motors position the carriage while the pen mechanism handles lift and contact.

STEP 04 / OUTPUT

Draw on paper

The machine traces the programmed path to create a physical result.
